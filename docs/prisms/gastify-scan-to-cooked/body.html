This page is assembled from two components, and that is its second lesson. Each half of gastify's line below is a self-contained fragment — its own floor, controls, gates, and inspectors — that any other page in this center can embed with one token. When the suite propagates to gastify, its doc pages can carry the intake story alone, or the kitchen loop alone, without this page existing there at all.

The numbers are invented. They are shaped like a plausible week so the line reads honestly — a bottleneck at the scan, a funnel at cooking — but nothing was measured. The measured page belongs in gastify's own command center, wired to its data. This one exists to teach the grammar.

What this example is, and is not

It is two things at once: the suite's domain example (a belt with a side input, a feedback loop, two human gates, and a named-gap ghost), and the demonstration of the component mode — an explanation page composed from embeddable fragments rather than one authored floor. It is not a status page for gastify: every number is invented (operator ruling 2026-08-01), it does not update when gastify changes, and the measured version gets built in gastify's own center at propagation, as its own page.

One consequence worth keeping in view: verify-prism.mjs passes these invented numbers exactly as it passed the ghosted ones — the gate checks a number is present, not traceable. The lede and the source line carry the honesty instead. That division of labour is recorded in the skill.
