ghost — an honest absence, never a zero

Waiting ten years costs $139,809, and only $24,000 of that is deposits you skipped — the other $115,809 is machine output that needed those years to exist and now cannot. No later rate or raise rebuilds it: +1 point recovers $54,078, less than half. Next move: start this month; time is the one lever you cannot buy back.

unpowered — it exists, and nothing turns it on

The same $72,000, deposited into a 0% checking account, is still $72,000 at year 30 — the machine is present and the engine never fires, $0/month forever. Waiting fixes nothing here. Next move: move the money to where a rate exists; the fix is a transfer, not patience.

The screen says $243,994; at 3% inflation those dollars buy $100,522 of today's prices — the readout claims wealth the machine did not create.
