4 The accountability split

Every rule the suite asserts lands in exactly one bucket. The two payable buckets are work someone can do; prompt-only rules are not failures, but they owe a disclosure carrier that puts them in front of the model at the moment they must fire. Every row opens the ledger filtered to that bucket.

8 The estate

What this center can answer today. The suite is not an application — there are no endpoints, models or coverage here. The spine is the lifecycle beat, and every lens filters by it.

LensStatusWhat it answers
BoardbuiltEvery open move the suite is carrying, projected from five committed sources — six framings, State is the landing one.
Enforcement ledgerbuiltEvery rule the suite asserts, classified by whether we can enforce it, do enforce it, or only claim to.
HooksbuiltThe six shipped hooks: slot, matcher, every reachable exit code, every fail-open path, and fixture coverage.
TestingbuiltThe fixture batteries that give every other gate its teeth — and the gates that have no battery at all.
AgentsbuiltThe subagent surface: which beats fork, which run read-only, and what the harness exposes.
Format methodsbuiltLiteral output-string contracts — the byte-identical strings, E1-E7 output shapes, trailers and glyph sets — and where they have drifted.
FunctionsbuiltEvery function the suite's own scripts define, across python, shell and mjs.
Data structuresbuiltEvery artifact shape the lifecycle reads or writes, and whether a validator stands behind it.
