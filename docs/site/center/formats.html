docs/src/mechanisms.md:38 argues byte-identical reuse exists precisely so “a plain-text diff can verify” the cross-file agreement — and no such diff ships. This page is that diff. Each contract's format is captured (not the prose that mentions it), reduced to a skeleton with placeholders blanked, and grouped. More than one skeleton means the carriers disagree.
