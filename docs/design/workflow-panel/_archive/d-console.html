Console — a portrait, a stat block, and a real command card

A, B and C are pure readouts — every row is text and no cell can be pressed. In StarCraft, Warcraft III and SC2 alike, the console's bottom-right corner holds the command card: two screen edges, the largest target, the highest click frequency in the game. A gives that corner to PLACE, B to a ledger column, C to a slot that clips.

D takes the RTS grammar literally: six unequal regions sized by what they hold. Hover any command cell — the four availability states are the answer to your requirement. A verb this element cannot use keeps its cell.
